Cyber Security Strategy Development AI case:

 

Brief

Your client is a regional enterprise undergoing cloud migration, digital expansion, and regulatory scrutiny. The CISO wants to use an AI agent to support cyber strategy development.

 

Design a use case for how the AI agent would support the cyber strategy team.

 

What the AI agent does

The AI agent acts as a cyber strategy analyst assistant, not a final decision-maker.

Core functions

Collect and summarize inputs
Reads audit findings, risk registers, past incidents, and business plans 
Extracts recurring themes such as IAM weakness, third-party exposure, resilience gaps, and cloud security issues 
Map issues to business risks
Connects cyber problems to business outcomes such as: 
customer trust 
operational disruption 
regulatory exposure 
revenue loss 
M&A integration risk 
Prioritize initiatives
Suggests which initiatives should come first based on: 
risk reduction 
cost 
implementation effort 
regulatory urgency 
alignment to business strategy 
Draft strategy options
Produces options such as: 
risk-reduction focused strategy 
resilience-focused strategy 
digital-growth enabling strategy 
Generate Board-ready outputs
Creates executive summaries 
Produces roadmaps 
Drafts KPIs/KRIs 
Prepares talking points for leadership
 

Output:

Well designed PowerPoint and/or HTML website.
